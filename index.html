Cheapest-ticket rules for India

  • Government buses (HRTC, RSRTC, UPSRTC, KSRTC, PRTC) are usually 30–50% cheaper than private sleepers on redBus. Ordinary buses sell at the counter, no booking needed.
  • Train sleeper class is the cheapest way to cover long distance overnight. Book 60 days ahead on IRCTC when you can.
  • Tatkal opens one day before travel: 10:00 for AC, 11:00 for sleeper. Log in at 09:55 with passenger details saved.
  • On a waitlisted ticket, check the prediction on ConfirmTkt or ixigo before you rely on it.
  • Flights: search on a Tuesday or Wednesday, compare with the airline's own site, and remember the airport bus or metro cost.

Before you leave the house

  • Save your ID in DigiLocker and keep one paper photocopy in a different bag.
  • Download offline Google Maps for every city on the route.
  • Split cash: some in wallet, some in your bag, ₹500 in a shoe. UPI fails in hills and on buses.
  • Share your live location and bus details with one person at home.
  • Screenshot every booking. Hostel check-in often asks for the ID you booked with.
  • Check the last bus time back from any day trip before you go.

🛡 Night-bus safety

Fill this before you board. It writes the message for your family or friend in one tap.

Open in WhatsApp ↗

  • Photograph the bus number plate before boarding and send it.
  • Keep your phone, cash and ID in a small bag on your body, not the overhead rack.
  • Women travelling alone: the seats just behind the driver are usually the safest. Call 112 or 181 if anything feels wrong.
  • Don't accept food or drinks from strangers on the bus.
