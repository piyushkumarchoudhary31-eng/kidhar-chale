Mountain passes and travel permits

When the high passes usually open, and which permits Indian travellers need. Snow changes things every year, so use this to plan and check live status before you go.

Last checked: 28 November 2026

Before any mountain trip: check the latest road status with the district administration, state police or the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) on social media, and ask your bus or taxi stand on the day.

📄 Permits for Indian travellers

WhereWhat you needHow to get it
Ladakh (Nubra, Pangong, Tso Moriri, Hanle)No inner line permit since 2021. Pay the environment fee: ₹400 once, plus ₹20 a day wildlife fee (₹50 Red Cross optional). Valid 21 days. Under-12s exempt.Online at lahdclehpermit.in; carry the printout and photo ID.
Rohtang Pass (Himachal)Vehicle permit ₹550 for sightseeing at the pass. Not needed if you go through the Atal Tunnel. Going beyond Sissu into Lahaul, non-HP vehicles pay a ₹200 (car) or ₹50 (bike) entry fee.Online at rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in; taxis in Manali arrange it.
SikkimNo permit to enter the state. A Protected Area Permit is needed for Tsomgo Lake, Nathu La, Zuluk, Dzongu and North Sikkim (Lachen, Lachung, Gurudongmar: minimum 3 days, maximum 5).Through a registered tour operator in Gangtok; carry photos and two photo IDs.
Arunachal PradeshInner Line Permit (ILP) for all visitors from outside the state.Official e-ILP portal (linked from the Arunachal tourism site), or liaison offices in Delhi, Kolkata and Guwahati.
NagalandInner Line Permit.ilp.nagaland.gov.in
MizoramInner Line Permit; temporary permits usually for 15 days, extendable.ilp.mizoram.gov.in
ManipurInner Line Permit; tourist permit valid 30 days. Kiosks at Imphal airport.manipurilponline.mn.gov.in
LakshadweepEntry permit for every visitor, usually with a sponsor or an approved booking.Through the Lakshadweep Administration.
Meghalaya, Assam, TripuraNo inner line permit. Meghalaya has been bringing in visitor registration, so check before you go.Not needed.

Sources: Ladakh permit guide (May 2026), Rohtang permit guide 2026, Sikkim permits 2026, Northeast ILP guide, Business Today, October 2026. Fees are set by each state and shown on the official portal before you pay.
