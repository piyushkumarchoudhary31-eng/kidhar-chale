Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Aswan?

Oct–Apr. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Aswan?

Train from Luxor.

How much does a trip to Aswan cost?

Daily cost: Cheap (under ₹2,500 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Egypt?

Apply online before you go (e-visa or ETA). e-Visa online, or on arrival if you hold a valid US, UK or Schengen visa. Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
