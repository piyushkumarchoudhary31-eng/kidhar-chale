Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Batanes?

Mar–Jun. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Batanes?

Flight from Manila.

How much does a trip to Batanes cost?

Daily cost: Moderate (₹2,500–5,000 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.
