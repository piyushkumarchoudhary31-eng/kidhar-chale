Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Chiang Mai?

Nov–Feb. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Chiang Mai?

Overnight train or bus from Bangkok.

How much does a trip to Chiang Mai cost?

Daily cost: Cheap (under ₹2,500 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Thailand?

Visa-free. For a short stay. Check the current number of days before you go. Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
