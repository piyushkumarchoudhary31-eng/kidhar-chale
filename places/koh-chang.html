Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Koh Chang?

Nov–Apr. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Koh Chang?

Bus from Bangkok to Trat, then ferry.

How much does a trip to Koh Chang cost?

Daily cost: Cheap (under ₹2,500 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.
