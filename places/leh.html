Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Leh?

May–Sep. These months have the best weather for a budget trip.

How do I reach Leh?

Bus from Manali or Srinagar; flights from Delhi.

How much does a trip to Leh cost?

About ₹1,490 per person for 3 days from Jammu on a tight budget, with government buses or sleeper trains, dorm beds and local food. Daily cost: Moderate: a mix of dorms, homestays and shared cabs. Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.
