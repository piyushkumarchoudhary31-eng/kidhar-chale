Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Lisbon?

Mar–Jun, Sep–Oct. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Lisbon?

Flights via the Gulf or Europe.

How much does a trip to Lisbon cost?

Daily cost: Pricey (over ₹5,000 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Portugal?

Visa needed. Schengen visa needed (covers most of Europe). Apply through VFS or BLS 2–3 months ahead. Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
