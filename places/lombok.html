Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Lombok?

May–Sep. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Lombok?

Ferry from Bali.

How much does a trip to Lombok cost?

Daily cost: Cheap (under ₹2,500 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Indonesia?

Visa on arrival. Visa on arrival (e-VOA online too). Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
