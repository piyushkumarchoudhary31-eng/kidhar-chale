Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Ninh Binh?

Jan–May, Sep–Nov. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Ninh Binh?

Bus or train from Hanoi.

How much does a trip to Ninh Binh cost?

Daily cost: Cheap (under ₹2,500 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.
