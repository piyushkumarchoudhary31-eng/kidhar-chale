Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Phobjikha?

Oct–Feb. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Phobjikha?

Taxi from Wangdue.

How much does a trip to Phobjikha cost?

Daily cost: Pricey (over ₹5,000 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Bhutan?

No visa needed. No visa, but you need an entry permit at the border and pay a daily fee (SDF) of ₹1,200 a night. Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
