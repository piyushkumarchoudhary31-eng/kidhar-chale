Quick answers

When is the best time to visit Split?

May–Oct. These months have the best weather; flights are usually cheaper outside school holidays.

How do I reach Split?

Bus or ferry along the coast.

How much does a trip to Split cost?

Daily cost: Moderate (₹2,500–5,000 a day). Real trips usually cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Do Indians need a visa for Croatia?

Visa needed. Schengen visa needed (covers most of Europe). Apply through VFS or BLS 2–3 months ahead. Checked November 2026; rules change often, so confirm on the official site before you book.
