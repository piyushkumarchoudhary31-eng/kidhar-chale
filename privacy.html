Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Kidhar Chale? is a free trip planner made by Piyush Choudhary. There are no ads, and you never have to sign up. An account is optional. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

If you don’t sign in, everything you type or plan is saved only in your own browser (in its local storage), on the device you are using. That includes your trips and plans, spends and splits, packing list, emergency card, passport and visa notes, language choice and places you looked at. It is never sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it.

To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app if you added it to your home screen.

If you tap Use my location, your browser asks you first. Your location is used on your phone to find the nearest city and its weather, and we don’t store it.

Buttons for buses, trains, stays and flights open other websites (like redBus, IRCTC or Booking.com). Once you are there, their own privacy rules apply.

Optional account (sign in with an email code)

You can sign in to keep your trips, money, lists and emergency card on every phone and laptop you use. You type your email, we email you a 6-digit code, and you type it back. There is no password.

  • Your email is never stored in readable form. We keep only a one-way scrambled key made from it (HMAC-SHA256 with a secret), so it can recognise you when you sign in again, but nobody can turn it back into your email.
  • Your name and saved data are encrypted before they are stored, in a private Google Sheet run by the site owner through Google Apps Script. The key is kept separately from the Sheet, so the Sheet alone shows only scrambled text.
  • Sign-in codes work for 10 minutes, are stored only as a scrambled hash, and are blocked after 5 wrong tries. Codes are sent from the site owner’s Gmail account by Google.
  • What is synced: your trips and current plan, money and splits, packing and other lists, emergency card and notes. Things that only make sense on one device (language, map view, photo cache) stay on that device.
  • Staying signed in: your browser keeps a random sign-in key for up to 90 days. You can sign out of one device, or of all devices, from the account panel.
  • Delete everything: tap your account button, then Delete my account. Your account and everything saved in it are removed from the Sheet straight away.

We don’t use your account for ads, don’t send newsletters, and don’t share it with anyone. Google runs the servers, so Google’s own security and privacy rules also apply.

Feedback and "what I spent" reports

If you choose to send feedback or share what a trip cost, we save what you type in a private Google Sheet run by the site owner, using Google Apps Script. Reports are shown on the site only as averages for a place, never with names. Your email or Instagram is optional and only used to reply to you. Ask us any time and we will delete what you sent.

Shared trip links

When you share a trip link, the whole plan is packed inside the link itself. It is not stored on any server. Anyone with the link can open the plan, so only send it to people you want to see it.

Partner links

Some links to hotel sites like Booking.com or Agoda may be partner links. If you book through them we may earn a small fee, at no extra cost to you. Those sites see that you came from Kidhar Chale?, as they would with any link.

Cookies and visit counting

Kidhar Chale? itself sets no cookies. To learn which features people use, we may use Google Analytics, but only if you say yes on the banner. If you say no, it never loads.

If you allow it, Google Analytics counts visits and actions like “made a plan” or “opened a booking site”. It does not get your plans, notes, names or spends. It sets these cookies:

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the site. Google’s own rules are at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The site is meant for travellers aged 13 and up. We don’t knowingly collect anything about children. You need to be 13 or older to create an account.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws, you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. If you have an account, you can see your saved trips and delete the account and everything in it yourself, any time, from the account panel. Without an account, your data is only on your device for you to view or clear. For anything else, contact us below.
