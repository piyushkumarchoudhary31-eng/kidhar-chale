Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Kidhar Chale? is a free trip planner made by Piyush Choudhary. There are no accounts, no ads and nothing to sign up for. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type or plan is saved only in your own browser (in its local storage), on the device you are using. That includes your trips and plans, spends and splits, packing list, emergency card, passport and visa notes, language choice and places you looked at. It is never sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it.

To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app if you added it to your home screen.

What goes online

A few features need the internet. When you use them, your browser talks directly to these services, and they see your IP address the way any website does:

ServiceWhyWhat it gets
Open-MeteoWeather for a placeThe place’s map position
OpenStreetMapMap pictures on ExploreWhich part of the map you look at
Google Fonts and cdnjsLetters and the map codeA normal file request
GitHub PagesHosts this websiteA normal page request

If you tap Use my location, your browser asks you first. Your location is used on your phone to find the nearest city and its weather, and we don’t store it.

Buttons for buses, trains, stays and flights open other websites (like redBus, IRCTC or Booking.com). Once you are there, their own privacy rules apply.

Cookies and visit counting

Kidhar Chale? itself sets no cookies. To learn which features people use, we may use Google Analytics, but only if you say yes on the banner. If you say no, it never loads.

If you allow it, Google Analytics counts visits and actions like “made a plan” or “opened a booking site”. It does not get your plans, notes, names or spends. It sets these cookies:

CookiePurposeKept for
_gaTells one visitor from another, without your name2 years
_ga_<ID>Remembers your visit session2 years

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the site. Google’s own rules are at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The site is meant for travellers aged 13 and up. We don’t knowingly collect anything about children, and the site does not ask for anyone’s age or name.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws, you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. We hold nothing on a server, and your own data is on your device for you to view or clear. For anything else, contact us below.
