Travel more on a student budget

Every rupee saved on the ticket is one more day on the road. Here is what actually works for students in India, with links to the official rules.

Last checked: 28 November 2026

🚆 Trains: the student concession

  • 50% off Sleeper and Second class for students travelling home or on educational tours (75% for SC/ST students). You need a concession certificate signed by your college or school.
  • The concession works only on counter (I-) tickets, not on IRCTC e-tickets. You can book a normal ticket first, then exchange it at a reservation counter with the certificate; the difference is refunded minus a ₹20 clerkage charge.
  • Monthly season tickets at half price for students, for daily travel up to 150 km between home and college.
  • For short hops, buy unreserved tickets on the official UTS app instead of standing in the queue.

Source: erail summary of the Indian Railways student concession rules, Indian Railways general rules for concession (PDF).

🏛 Monuments and museums

  • Children under 15 enter ASI monuments free, Indian or foreign. The Taj Mahal costs ₹50 for Indians (₹200 more for the main tomb) and is closed every Friday.
  • Free entry days at ASI monuments: World Heritage Day (18 April), International Museum Day (18 May) and World Heritage Week (19 to 25 November). Plan Agra or Delhi trips around them.
  • Many state-run museums and forts sell cheaper student tickets. Always carry your college ID card and ask at the counter.

Sources: Taj Mahal official ticketing, AIR News on free entry days, Outlook Traveller on World Heritage Week.

🛏 Cheap and free places to sleep

  • YHAI youth hostels: a membership gets you cheap dorm beds across India and some of the cheapest guided treks. yhaindia.org
  • Gurudwaras offer free langar to everyone, and many have sarais (rooms) for pilgrims for a donation. Be respectful: cover your head and follow the rules.
  • Dharamshalas and ashrams in pilgrim towns like Rishikesh, Haridwar, Varanasi and Pushkar give clean, very cheap rooms.
  • Hostel volunteering: many backpacker hostels give a free bed and meals for a few hours of work a day. Read the terms before you agree.
  • Overnight buses and trains save a night of stay. Our planner uses them when you tick "Overnight is fine".

🚌 Getting around for less

  • State government buses (HRTC, RSRTC, KSRTC, UPSRTC and others) are usually the cheapest way between towns, and many now sell tickets online.
  • In the mountains, shared jeeps and sumos leave from fixed stands and cost a fraction of a private taxi.
  • Split cabs, scooters and rooms with friends. The Money tool works out who owes whom.

🍛 Food and money

  • Eat where locals eat: thalis, dhabas, college canteens and temple or gurudwara langar.
  • Carry a bottle and refill it with filtered water to save on bottled water.
  • UPI works almost everywhere, but keep some cash for hills and villages where the network drops and ATMs are far apart.

🏔 Ladakh tip

Indian visitors to Ladakh pay an online environment fee instead of an inner line permit. Students can ask at the Leh DC office about a reduced student fee. See passes and permits for details.
